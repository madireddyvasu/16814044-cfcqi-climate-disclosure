# Research questions and hypotheses

## Research questions

1. What is the level and pattern of quantified and financially connected climate disclosure among the sampled climate-sensitive FTSE 350 firms across two reporting cycles?
2. How are firm characteristics—particularly firm size, external sustainability assurance and board-level sustainability/climate oversight—associated with CFCQI?
3. Is there a systematic within-firm change in CFCQI across the two reporting cycles, and which dimensions show the largest remaining gaps?

## Hypotheses for RQ2

- **H01:** Firm size has no statistically significant association with CFCQI.
- **H11:** Firm size is positively associated with CFCQI.
- **H02:** External sustainability assurance has no statistically significant association with CFCQI.
- **H12:** Firms obtaining qualifying external sustainability assurance have higher CFCQI scores.
- **H03:** Board-level sustainability/climate oversight has no statistically significant association with CFCQI.
- **H13:** Firms with qualifying board-level sustainability/climate oversight have higher CFCQI scores.

Hypotheses are evaluated using the sign, magnitude, confidence interval and p-value of the corresponding pooled OLS coefficient.
