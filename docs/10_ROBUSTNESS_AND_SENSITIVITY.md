# Robustness and sensitivity

The repository preserves all pre-specified and audit-added robustness checks:

- pooled OLS with firm-clustered standard errors
- variance inflation factors
- residual and heteroskedasticity diagnostics
- leverage / Cook's-distance influence review
- exclusion of Mobico's atypical 15-month period
- exclusion of Tesco's 53-week period
- combined Mobico + Tesco exclusion
- sensitivity to the F009 (Drax Group) board-oversight classification
- latest-cycle-only HC3 regression
- paired t-test and Wilcoxon evidence for RQ3
- item-level latest/prior change analysis

The most stable determinant is board-level sustainability/climate oversight. The pooled firm-size relationship is positive but should be described with a sensitivity caveat because it weakens in the 40-observation latest-cycle model.
