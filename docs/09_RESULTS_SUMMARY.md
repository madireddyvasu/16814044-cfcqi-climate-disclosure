# Results summary

## Dataset and audit
- 40 firms / 80 firm-year observations
- 80 verified source reports / 80 unique SHA-256 hashes
- 960 CFCQI decisions
- Mean CFCQI: **51.77** (SD **18.65**)
- All core integrity checks pass

## RQ1
Energy has the highest group mean (**62.91**). The strongest dimensions are financial-planning integration (83.8% scoring 2) and quantified scenario analysis (62.5% scoring 2). The weakest are current financial position (77.5% scoring 0), current financial performance (68.8% scoring 0) and current cash flows (72.5% scoring 0).

## RQ2
| Predictor | Coefficient | p-value | Primary conclusion |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Firm size | 4.60 | 0.018 | Positive in pooled model; weaker in latest-only HC3 |
| External assurance | 11.17 | 0.185 | Not statistically supported |
| Board oversight | 23.76 | <0.001 | Strong positive association; robust across sensitivities |

Primary model R² = **0.478**. The latest-only HC3 model retains a strong board-oversight association while the firm-size p-value rises to approximately 0.076.

## RQ3
Mean Latest − Prior change is approximately **−0.003 CFCQI points**; median change is 0. The Wilcoxon p-value is **0.896**, indicating no systematic overall change. Excluding Mobico, Tesco, or both does not change the conclusion.
