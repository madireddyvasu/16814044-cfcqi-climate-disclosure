# CFCQI Coding Manual — FROZEN v1.0 (2026-10-07)

**Study:** Quantified and Financially Connected Climate Disclosure in Climate-Sensitive FTSE 350 Firms (Vasu Deva Rao Madireddy, 7015SOM, Route 1)
**Status:** FROZEN — do not change after this point. (v0.1 was the pilot draft, used for the 5-report pilot on 2026-10-07.)
**Derived from:** UK SRS S2 / IFRS S2 climate financial-effects concepts (IFRS S2 paras 15–24): current and anticipated effects on financial position, financial performance and cash flows; capital deployment; financial planning integration; scenario analysis.

---

## 1. Unit of analysis and source documents

- Unit: **firm–report–year** (one row per annual report; 2 rows per firm).
- Primary source: the annual report (integrated AR where published). Sustainability/climate reports count **only** where the annual report cross-references them directly (e.g. "see our Climate Report p.12 for further detail") — no independent browsing.
- Only count disclosures **within the financial-reporting connectivity frame**: governance-only or emissions-only statements with no financial connection score 0.
- Cycle labels: **T** = most recent annual report available at download; **T1** = the prior consecutive annual report.

## 2. Scoring scale (applies to every item)

| Score | Meaning | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | No relevant financial connection | Item absent, or discussed with no link to a financial consequence |
| **1** | Qualitative / generic financial connection | Financial consequence mentioned but not quantified (e.g. "may impact profitability", "material capex expected") |
| **2** | Quantified financial effect OR specific financial-statement connection | A £ figure, % of revenue/cost, quantified provision/impairment, a specific balance-sheet line item affected, an assumption disclosed in the financial statements (e.g. useful-life change, discount rate), or an explicit link to a budget/plan |

**Tie-break rules**
- If both a qualitative and a quantified statement exist for the same item in one report, score the higher (2).
- Carbon-emissions volume alone (tCO₂e) with no £ implication scores **0** on every item — volume ≠ financial effect.
- **"No material impact" rule (pilot refinement):** an explicit statement that climate testing/assessment found no material financial-statement impact scores **1** (not 0) — it evidences the assessment was performed. Example: "scenario analyses identified no material financial impact to the current year impairment assessments."
- **Dual scoring permitted (pilot refinement):** the same evidence may support multiple items; score each item on its own definition. Example: scenario-quantified asset effects may score under both Item 5 and Item 12; carbon-cost exposures may score under both Item 6 and Item 10.
- Forward-looking quantified commitments (e.g. "£500m transition capex by 2030") score under the relevant anticipated/capex item — count as 2 (specific financial commitment).

## 3. The 12 CFCQI items

### Item 1 — Current financial performance (past/current year)
**Definition:** Disclosed effect of climate matters on revenue, operating costs, gross/operating profit or margins in the reporting period.
- 0: nothing relevant.
- 1: "climate-related costs affected margins" (no figure).
- 2: £ figure or % (e.g. "extreme weather disrupted production, costing £12m" / "carbon costs reduced EBIT by 3%").

### Item 2 — Current financial position
**Definition:** Effect on assets, liabilities or equity in the reporting period's balance sheet.
- 0: nothing relevant.
- 1: "asset values are exposed to climate risk" (no figure/item).
- 2: £ figure, % of carrying value, or named balance-sheet item (e.g. "£45m decommissioning provision recognised" / "flood-exposed inventory written down by £8m" / **"12% of carrying value of physical assets exposed to climate-related physical risks" — quantified exposure as a % of the asset base counts as 2 even without a £ figure**).

### Item 3 — Current cash flows
**Definition:** Effect on operating/investing/financing cash flows in the reporting period.
- 0: nothing relevant.
- 1: "cash flows were affected by energy costs" (no figure).
- 2: £ figure (e.g. "£20m capex paid on decarbonisation projects in-year" / "operating cash outflow included £6m carbon-allowance purchases").

### Item 4 — Anticipated effects on future financial performance
**Definition:** Forward-looking statements linking climate risks/opportunities to future revenue, costs or profit (short/medium/long term).
- 0: nothing relevant.
- 1: "transition risks may affect future profitability".
- 2: £ or % range (e.g. "we expect transition costs of £30–40m p.a. by 2030" / "carbon pricing could add 2–4% to operating costs").

### Item 5 — Anticipated effects on future financial position
**Definition:** Forward-looking links to future assets, liabilities or equity (e.g. stranded assets, asset retirements, future provisions).
- 0: nothing relevant.
- 1: "certain assets may become stranded" (no figure).
- 2: £ figure or item (e.g. "£150m of coal-exposed assets at risk of impairment" / "planned £200m remediation provision over 2028–32").

### Item 6 — Anticipated effects on future cash flows
**Definition:** Forward-looking links to future cash generation or outflows.
- 0: nothing relevant.
- 1: "future cash flows may be volatile under transition scenarios".
- 2: £ figure (e.g. "expected cumulative transition spend of £1.2bn to 2030" / "scenario modelling shows a £90m annual cash shortfall under a disorderly transition").

### Item 7 — Capital expenditure and capital allocation
**Definition:** Climate-related capex plans, capex alignment figures (e.g. taxonomy-aligned or transition-capex %), or investment commitments.
- 0: nothing relevant.
- 1: "significant investment will be required" (no figure).
- 2: £ figure or % of capex (e.g. "£250m transition capex committed for 2027" / "38% of 2026 capex aligned with our net-zero pathway").

### Item 8 — Asset valuations, impairment and useful lives
**Definition:** Specific connection to financial-statement measurement: impairment testing assumptions, useful-life/residual-value changes, fair-value disclosures referencing climate.
- 0: nothing relevant.
- 1: "climate considered in impairment reviews" (no specifics).
- 2: named assumption or item (e.g. "useful lives of refining assets reduced from 25 to 18 years" / "impairment model uses a 1.5°C carbon-price path of £85/t").

### Item 9 — Provisions, contingencies and commitments
**Definition:** Climate-related provisions, contingent liabilities or contractual commitments with financial effect.
- 0: nothing relevant.
- 1: "we may face remediation obligations" (no figure).
- 2: £ figure (e.g. "£60m provision for site remediation" / "contingent liability disclosed at £25m for pending emissions litigation").
- **Pilot refinement:** a specific provision/contingency figure is required for 2 — generic assumption lists do not score. An auditor's Key Audit Matter mentioning climate in provisions is **not** a management disclosure and scores 0 unless management itself names or quantifies the provision.

### Item 10 — Carbon pricing, ETS and regulatory cost quantification
**Definition:** Quantified or described financial effect of carbon prices, emissions-trading schemes, carbon taxes, offsets or related regulation.
- 0: nothing relevant.
- 1: "exposure to the UK ETS may increase costs".
- 2: £ figure (e.g. "UK ETS compliance cost £18m in 2026" / "offset purchases totalled £4m").

### Item 11 — Financial planning and budget integration
**Definition:** Explicit link between climate strategy and the financial planning process: budgets, business plans, medium-term financial targets or capital-allocation frameworks.
- 0: nothing relevant.
- 1: "climate is integrated into our financial planning" (no specifics).
- 2: specific linkage (e.g. "the 2027–29 business plan includes £400m decarbonisation funding" / "executive incentive scorecard weights emissions targets at 20%").
- **Pilot refinement:** a **standing mechanism or decision rule** counts as 2 (e.g. an internal carbon price applied to all investment decisions; the operating plan includes carbon-cost forecasts; a climate target sits in the executive bonus scorecard). A **one-off analysis** or generic "climate is considered in planning" counts as 1.

### Item 12 — Quantified scenario analysis with financial outcomes
**Definition:** Climate scenario analysis reporting quantified financial outcomes (not just narrative scenarios).
- 0: no scenario analysis, or narrative only with no financial figure.
- 1: scenarios described with qualitative financial implications ("under a 2°C scenario, margins would be pressured").
- 2: £ or % financial outcomes under stated scenarios (e.g. "under a disorderly-transition scenario, NPV of the portfolio falls by £310m"). A quantified sensitivity on the key climate input (e.g. carbon-tax sensitivity on impairment) counts as 2.

## 4. CFCQI computation

- Raw score = Σ(items 1–12) → range 0–24.
- **CFCQI = (raw / 24) × 100** → range 0–100.
- A CFCQI of 62.5 means 62.5% of the maximum research-instrument score — **NOT** regulatory compliance.

## 5. Firm-level variables (extract once per firm–year)

| Variable | Coding |
|---|---|
| firm_id | unique firm code (F001–F040) |
| firm_name | as listed in the sample register |
| tcfd_group | Energy / Transportation / Materials & Buildings / Agriculture, Food & Forest Products |
| cycle | T (most recent) / T1 (prior consecutive year) |
| size | ln(total assets in £m, from the balance sheet). **FX rule (pilot refinement):** non-GBP reporters convert at the ECB euro foreign exchange reference rate on the balance-sheet date; record the rate and source in notes. Reference: 31 Dec 2025 — USD→GBP 0.74264 (ECB EUR/USD 1.1750, EUR/GBP 0.87260), EUR→GBP 0.87260. |
| assurance | 1 = external assurance over sustainability/environmental subject matter obtained (limited or reasonable, even on selected metrics only); 0 = none. The statutory financial-statement audit alone = 0. |
| board_oversight | 1 = dedicated board-level committee OR named board committee with an explicit sustainability/climate/environment remit in its described role (e.g. "evaluates policies for handling current and emerging HSES risks, including climate-related risks"); 0 = none. Full-board oversight without such a committee = 0; management-level committees = 0. |
| leverage | total debt / total assets (total debt = interest-bearing borrowings, current + non-current) |
| roa | net income attributable to owners / total assets |

## 6. Evidence and audit trail (pilot refinement)

- For **every** item score, record in `data/interim/evidence/<firm_id>_<cycle>.md`: the score, a short **verbatim evidence quote**, and the **printed page number** as shown in the report's own footer/contents pagination (not the PDF-viewer page number).
- Log ambiguous cases in `docs/decision_log.md`.
- If a report is unreadable or a variable genuinely cannot be found, write `MISSING:<reason>` in notes and continue — **never invent a number**.
- Score **only** from the document in front of you — never from memory of the company.

## 7. Reliability procedure (per proposal)

- 5-report pilot → refine rules → **freeze codebook** (this document).
- After a coding interval, randomly re-code **15% of reports**; compute **percentage agreement** and **weighted Cohen's κ** on item scores. Target: κ ≥ 0.60 (substantial) before proceeding to analysis.
