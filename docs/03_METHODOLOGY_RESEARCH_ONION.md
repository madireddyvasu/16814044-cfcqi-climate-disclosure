# Methodology and Research Onion alignment

The final methodology follows the Research Onion sequence used for the technical implementation:

1. **Philosophy — Pragmatism:** selected because the project is driven by an applied reporting problem and combines documentary measurement with statistical decision support.
2. **Approach — Deductive:** research questions, CFCQI dimensions and hypotheses are specified before inferential analysis.
3. **Methodological choice — Mono-method quantitative:** annual-report text is transformed into numeric variables through predefined rules; the project does not perform thematic analysis.
4. **Strategy — Deductive quantitative content analysis:** a fixed 12-item CFCQI is applied to complete annual reports.
5. **Time horizon — Two-period repeated firm-year design:** each firm contributes a prior and latest annual report.
6. **Techniques/procedures — Data collection, CFCQI scoring, firm-variable extraction, reliability, OLS, diagnostics, paired tests and robustness.**

## Why this is not thematic analysis

The project does not generate emergent qualitative themes. The categories are predefined, each report is reviewed against a frozen codebook, and each dimension receives a numeric score of 0, 1 or 2. The resulting dataset is analysed quantitatively.

## Equal weighting

All 12 CFCQI dimensions are equally weighted because no validated empirical or regulatory basis was identified for differential weights. Equal weighting is transparent, reproducible and prevents post-hoc weight selection based on the observed results.
